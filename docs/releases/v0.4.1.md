# ArzDesk v0.4.1

- One account entry in the header opens a right-hand drawer. The duplicate sidebar Profile link is removed.
- The drawer contains Settings (name, confirmed email change, password) and explicit Logout. It supports Escape, backdrop dismissal, focus return, keyboard access and small screens.
- Account settings use filled sections without perimeter outlines or equal-height empty cards. Workspace panels also lose their perimeter border.
- Sign-in has an open doorway; sign-up has building blocks; password recovery has an envelope/key; password reset has a shield/lock. Each has its own headline, explanation and caption. Purpose-specific text and artwork remain visible on phones.
- The cedar control inverts its own colors with the theme and animates a direct pull/flip. The company logo and favicon keep the original asset. Light/Dark only; keyboard and reduced-motion support remain.
- No dependencies, database schema or business permissions changed.

## Upgrade

Preserve `.env` and PostgreSQL data. Stop the server, overwrite source contents, clear `.next`, then restart. From v0.3.0 or later, no dependency reinstall, migration or seed is needed. See [Windows instructions](../../UPDATE_AR.md).

## Validation

Production build, ESLint, 28 unit/database/auth tests and browser flows cover workspace permissions, account name/email/password updates, session revocation, drawer dismissal and Logout, unique authentication scenes and responsive layouts in both themes.
